Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known place in the
universe confirmed to host life. About 71% of Earth's surface is covered
by water, mostly in oceans. Earth's atmosphere is composed mainly of
nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%), which supports the vast diversity of
life on the planet.

Earth completes one rotation on its axis every 24 hours and one orbit
around the Sun every 365.25 days. Its axial tilt of about 23.5 degrees is
responsible for the planet's seasons. Earth has one natural satellite,
the Moon, which stabilizes the planet's tilt and influences ocean tides.
