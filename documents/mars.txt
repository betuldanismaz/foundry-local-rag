Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, often called the "Red Planet"
because of the iron oxide (rust) that covers much of its surface. Mars
orbits at an average distance of about 228 million kilometers (1.52
astronomical units) from the Sun. Mars has the largest volcano in the
Solar System, Olympus Mons, which stands about 22 kilometers tall —
nearly three times the height of Mount Everest.

Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos, which are thought to be
captured asteroids. A Martian day (called a "sol") is about 24 hours and
37 minutes long, very close to an Earth day. Evidence from rovers and
orbiters suggests Mars once had liquid water on its surface, making it a
major focus of the search for past or present life beyond Earth.
