Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second largest in the
Solar System. It is best known for its spectacular ring system, made up
mostly of ice particles with smaller amounts of rocky debris and dust.
The rings extend up to 282,000 kilometers from the planet but are
remarkably thin, generally less than a kilometer thick.

Like Jupiter, Saturn is a gas giant composed mainly of hydrogen and
helium, and it is the least dense planet in the Solar System — it would
float in water if there were an ocean big enough to hold it. Saturn has
over 140 confirmed moons, the largest of which, Titan, has a thick
atmosphere and lakes of liquid methane on its surface.
