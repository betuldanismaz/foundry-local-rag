Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System and the closest to the
Sun. It completes one orbit around the Sun every 88 Earth days, the
shortest year of any planet. Despite its proximity to the Sun, Mercury is
not the hottest planet — that distinction belongs to Venus, because
Mercury has almost no atmosphere to trap heat.

Mercury's surface is heavily cratered, similar in appearance to Earth's
Moon. Daytime temperatures can reach 430 degrees Celsius, while nighttime
temperatures can drop to -180 degrees Celsius, the largest temperature
swing of any planet in the Solar System.
