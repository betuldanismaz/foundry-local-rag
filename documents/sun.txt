The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System. It is a nearly
perfect sphere of hot plasma, formed about 4.6 billion years ago from the
gravitational collapse of a giant molecular cloud. The Sun accounts for
about 99.86% of the total mass of the Solar System.

The Sun's surface temperature is about 5,500 degrees Celsius, while its
core reaches about 15 million degrees Celsius. Nuclear fusion in the core
converts hydrogen into helium, releasing the energy that powers sunlight
and heat. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to
reach Earth.
