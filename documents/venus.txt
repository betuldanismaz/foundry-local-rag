Venus is the second planet from the Sun and Earth's closest planetary
neighbor. It is often called Earth's "sister planet" because of their
similar size and mass, but the two planets have very different
atmospheres. Venus has a thick atmosphere composed mostly of carbon
dioxide, with clouds of sulfuric acid.

This dense atmosphere traps heat through an extreme greenhouse effect,
making Venus the hottest planet in the Solar System with a surface
temperature of about 465 degrees Celsius — hot enough to melt lead.
Venus rotates very slowly and in the opposite direction to most other
planets, so the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.
