Acme Widgets Co. Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager
approval. Remote work requests must be submitted through the HR portal at
least one week in advance. Employees are expected to be available during
core hours, 10 AM to 3 PM local time, regardless of work location.

All remote employees must use company-issued laptops with full-disk
encryption enabled and must connect to internal systems only through the
approved VPN client. Violations of this policy may result in revocation
of remote work privileges.
